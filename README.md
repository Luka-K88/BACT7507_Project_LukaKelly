# BACT7507_Project_LukaKelly
## This repository contains the idea for my semester project
Part 1 Root Cause Analysis Using 5 Whys:
Problem Statement:
Musicians struggle to find people of relevant skill levels to grow and learn from, this slows the development of their skills.

5 Whys:
Problem: Musicians struggle to find people to collaborate with.
Why 1: There is disconnection in the community
Why 2: It will help people grow in their skills
Why 3: It will encourage people to collaborate
Why 4: It will make playing music more enjoyable
Why 5: It will allow more people to become professional musicians

## Part 4 Assessment Draft Preparation:
Refined Problem Statement:
Musicians often struggle to find other musicians of relevant skill level to collaborate with due to disconnection in the music community. This project aims to increase the skills and abilities of musicians.

Root Cause:
The root cause of this issue is due to their being no centralized way for musicians to connect, although there are platforms such as Instagram, discord and others, these focus more on communication and content sharing not collaboration. Due to musicians relying on personal connections, often people new to music are disconnected from others that match their style, genre and skill level. This project aims to create a space for musicians to grow effectively.

Stakeholders:
1.	Musicians
2.	Developers
3.	Lecturer
4.	Audio Engineers/Producers

Feasibility:
This project has high feasibility due to there being no paid resources needed for development, and it uses relatively basic coding languages such as JavaScript, HTML and CSS.  It is realistic to finish initial development in 12 weeks with additional features being added as necessary.
